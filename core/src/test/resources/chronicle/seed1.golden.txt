== HOUSE_FOUNDED (event 0)
  A tavern rumour: The year 1 saw Serora plant a flayed hand on red in the black soil and name it House Sallow.
  Cantor Moric of House Sallow: In the year 1 Serora the Prudent took up a flayed hand on red and swore "Every Wound A Verse" upon the bones of the old. So House Sallow began, and so it endures.
  Courtier Ysere of House Quietus: House Sallow was born in the year 1, the way a blister is born: from friction, and of no use to anyone.
== HOUSE_FOUNDED (event 1)
  An unsigned broadsheet: In the year 1, House Quietus raised a banner of a lidless eye sewn shut on ash grey and called itself a house.
  Courtier Rena of House Quietus: In the year 1 Aesent the Fierce took up a lidless eye sewn shut on ash grey and swore "Silence Is Owed" upon the bones of the old. So House Quietus began, and so it endures.
  Gnawer Skent of House Gullet: They say House Quietus was founded in the year 1. Founded is a generous word for it.

== MEGABEAST_STIRS (event 13)
  An unsigned broadsheet: In the year 2 Old Hunger the Brass Titan rose out of the dark and made the deep strata its own.
  Herald Gharnesh of The Antlered Host: In the year 2 the deep gave us Old Hunger the Brass Titan. Let our enemies be the ones who meet it.
  A shelter-keeper's ledger: In the year 2 Old Hunger the Brass Titan woke, and every house of the Maze began to pray it would eat someone else.
== MEGABEAST_RAID (event 14)

  Cantor Sibeth of House Sallow: The deep visited House Scourgehall in the year 4. Old Hunger the Brass Titan left little to bury. The ditches were full for a month.
  Cantor Cindius of House Scourgehall: In the year 4 House Scourgehall fought Old Hunger the Brass Titan in its own halls, and lost more than it can say.
