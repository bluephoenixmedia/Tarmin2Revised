== HOUSE_FOUNDED (event 0)
  A tavern rumour: The year 1 saw Serora plant a flayed hand on red in the black soil and name it House Sallow.
  Cantor Moric of House Sallow: In the year 1 Serora the Prudent took up a flayed hand on red and swore "Every Wound A Verse" upon the bones of the old. So House Sallow began, and so it endures.
  Courtier Ysere of House Quietus: House Sallow was born in the year 1, the way a blister is born: from friction, and of no use to anyone.
