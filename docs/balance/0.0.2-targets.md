# 0.0.2 balance targets

The numbers 0.0.2 is tuned to. Each line is an index entry: the decision and its reasoning live in the linked GitHub issue on `bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised`, and that issue wins if this page disagrees. Produced by the 0.0.2 polish map ([#149](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/149)).

**Reference:** a fresh Incarnation on **MEDIUM** (the only difficulty played), unarmoured, carrying the Father's Sword, played by a roguelike-literate player. Terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md`.

**Measure with:**
- `./gradlew :lwjgl3:runDuelSimulation -PduelArgs="--minLevel=1 --maxLevel=1"` for single fights ([#175](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/175));
- `python tools/telemetry/aggregate.py` for whole expeditions ([#154](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/154)).

## Baseline before tuning

- **Telemetry:** 3 lives, all dead by depth 2. Player hit rate 86%; monsters out-acted the player 41 attacks to 14. ([#155](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/155))
- **Simulator, depth-1 band:** 70.5% wins, 36% HP lost on a win, 3 swings to kill, 72% monster hit rate, a median of 1 fight survived back to back. ([#175](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/175))

## Combat ([#158](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/158), work in [#177](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/177))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| One depth-1 fight | about 95% won, 20-35% HP lost |
| Fights before a real heal | 2-3 (3-5 once Vengeance is in) |
| Swings to kill | 2-4 at depth 1 (4-6 accepted with the Father's Sword); 3-6 at matched level; 8-15 for elites and bosses |
| Player full-hit rate | 65-75% at matched level, never below 55%. Glancing blows are not hits. |
| Monster hit rate | 55-65% against an unarmoured player; armour takes 10-20 points off |
| Speed | capped at 16 at depths 1-2; damage cut in proportion |
| Resistance | Resistant ⅔ damage, Weak 1.5×, crits pierce ([#164](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/164)) |

Starting levers: MEDIUM HP about 22, to-hit +4, monster stat floor 0, Father's Sword 1d3 keepsake (+3 max HP, +5% crit, deepening per Incarnation).

### Exposure as built ([#194](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/194))

All in `gamedata/player/Exposure.java`; `ExposureTest` pins the pacing.

- **Bands (body °C):** Hot >38 (heatstroke, unchanged), Warm ≥37.25, Comfortable ≥36, Chilly ≥35 (warning), Cold ≥34 (-10% speed), Freezing ≥32.5 (-20%, no natural regen), Hypothermic <32.5 (-20%, no regen, 1 HP every 10 turns, 5% a turn to lose an action). Floor 30.
- **Heat loss:** "feels like" = air + worn warmth. Below 15 the body loses 0.0008 °C per degree of shortfall per turn (×2 WET, ×3 SOAKED); at or above it, it recovers 0.02 a turn toward 37. Above 35 air, heat gain as before.
- **Surface air:** Forest 20, Lakelands 16, Blight 16, Ocean 19, Mountains 4, Tundra -12; every biome but the Desert (own curve) up to 6 colder at night. Clear +2, fog -1, rain -5, storm -6 (heavy or extreme -1 more); snow and blizzard as before. Under a shelter roof at least 14 and no rain.
- **Strata air:** Maze 18, Fungal Forest 18, Ossuary 12, Flooded Halls 6, Magma Deeps 36; forge and pyre gashes 26, Glass Gullet 8, other gashes 15.
- **Measured pacing (rags only):** Tundra by day turns Cold at about 110 turns; a wet night in the Forest rain at about 310; wet in the Flooded Halls at about 180. A cloak answers the last two outright.
- **Wetness (0-100):** WET ≥10, SOAKED ≥60. Rain 0.8 a turn (storm 1.5, snow 0.3, blizzard 0.6) × (1 - coverage), never past WET. Wading sets 40; Freezing Slush, or water in Tundra or Mountains at night or at freezing air, sets 100 and drops 1.5 °C on the way in. A draught of slush: -1 °C and WET. Dries 0.2 a turn, 4 by a fire or Molten Fire, 6 at a hearth.
- **Heat:** a field fire or campfire within 2 tiles warms 0.15 a turn to 37.5; a lit shelter hearth 0.4; a lit carried lantern is +3 warmth; Molten Fire within 1 tile is 45 °C air.
- **Field fire:** a Tinder Bundle (or Firewood with no fire near) used in the field; burns 100 turns, Firewood adds 100 (cap 300); allows cooking within 2 tiles; every 5 turns, monsters with line of sight within 8 tiles come to look. Not saved.
- **Clothing (`armor.json` `warmthBonus`, `coverage`):** rags 2 (built in, no coverage). Cloak 8 / coverage 0.75 (Cloak, Cloak of Shadows); Toxic Veil 3 / 0.3; Hood of Clarity 2 / 0.25. Body armour 4 (mail and plate), 5 leather, studded, gallic and brigandine, 6 hide and padded, 3 cord, wood/bone, hoplite and rusty, 2 tabard. Legs and boots 2 (hide, padded 3). Arms 1 (hide 2). Helmets 1 (leather, hide, padded, studded, gallic and the plain Helmet 2). Gauntlets 1. Shields, amulets, belts and the visor 0. Ring of Warmth +20.

| Gold | NetHack rarity: floor piles in about a third of rooms, `rnd(depth+2) × rnd(30)`; some monsters carry gold |
| Divinity | 15-25 per depth-1 expedition; first station after expedition 1-2 |
