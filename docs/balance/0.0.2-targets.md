# 0.0.2 balance targets

The numbers 0.0.2 is tuned to. Each line is an index entry: the decision and its reasoning live in the linked GitHub issue on `bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised`, and that issue wins if this page disagrees. Produced by the 0.0.2 polish map ([#149](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/149)).

**Reference:** a fresh Incarnation on **MEDIUM** (the only difficulty played), unarmoured, carrying the Father's Sword, played by a roguelike-literate player. Terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md`.

**Measure with:**
- `./gradlew :lwjgl3:runDuelSimulation -PduelArgs="--minLevel=1 --maxLevel=1"` for single fights ([#175](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/175));
- `python tools/telemetry/aggregate.py` for whole expeditions ([#154](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/154)).

## Baseline before tuning

- **Telemetry:** 3 lives, all dead by depth 2. Player hit rate 86%; monsters out-acted the player 41 attacks to 14. ([#155](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/155))
- **Simulator, depth-1 band:** 70.5% wins, 36% HP lost on a win, 3 swings to kill, 72% monster hit rate, a median of 1 fight survived back to back. ([#175](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/175))

## Combat ([#158](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/158), work in [#177](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/177))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| One depth-1 fight | about 95% won, 20-35% HP lost |
| Fights before a real heal | 2-3 (3-5 once Vengeance is in) |
| Swings to kill | 2-4 at depth 1 (4-6 accepted with the Father's Sword); 3-6 at matched level; 8-15 for elites and bosses |
| Player full-hit rate | 65-75% at matched level, never below 55%. Glancing blows are not hits. |
| Monster hit rate | 55-65% against an unarmoured player; armour takes 10-20 points off |
| Speed | capped at 16 at depths 1-2; damage cut in proportion |
| Resistance | Resistant ⅔ damage, Weak 1.5×, crits pierce ([#164](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/164)) |

Starting levers: MEDIUM HP about 22, to-hit +4, monster stat floor 0, Father's Sword 1d3 keepsake (+3 max HP, +5% crit, deepening per Incarnation).

## Difficulty by depth ([#161](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/161), work in [#185](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/185))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| On-pace single-fight wins | 95% at depths 1-2, 90% at 3-5, 85% at 6+ |
| EDL | depth and distance only; never the player's level |
| Encounters | Fodder packs of 3-6, Soldiers, rare Heavies; density grows through groups |
| Out-of-depth monsters | rare, at most EDL+2, recognisable, bonus XP |

## Progression ([#162](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/162), superseded in part by [#176](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/176); work in [#183](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/183), [#184](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/184), [#187](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/187))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| First level-up | within the first expedition, after 6-10 depth-1 kills |
| Kills per level | 8-10 on-level kills at every level |
| Milestones | level 5 around depth 3-4; level 11 around the endgame strata |
| Death | permanent per Incarnation: level, stats and gear lost; Known Spells, the Father's Sword, unlocks, altar and chest kept |
| Unlocks | rolled at every expedition end; the guaranteed one on death |

## Tarmin's Hunger ([#176](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/176), work in [#188](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/188))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| End of the game | 50 deaths (the Bridge falls) |
| Stage from deaths | 2 at 10 deaths, 3 at 25, 4 at 40; turns within an expedition still escalate |
| Per-death damage scaling | none |

## Survival ([#159](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/159))

| System | Targets | Decision / work |
|---|---|---|
| Healing | Vengeance: Fodder 2%, Soldier 8%, Heavy 20% of max HP (+50% for out-of-depth monsters, nothing for monsters 3+ levels below). Regen interval 20 turns at level 1, about 8 at level 10. Poultice 30% over 5 turns. MP 1 per 12 turns at WIS 10. | [#178](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/178) / [#190](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/190) |
| Hunger | 900 to start, 1 per turn; Hungry <150, Weak <50, Fainting <0. A fed start lasts about 750 turns. 2 rations at the start. | [#179](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/179) / [#191](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/191) |
| Thirst | 900 to start, 1.5 per turn; drink about every 600 turns. Blood pools about 70% illness. Waterskin holds 5 drinks. | [#180](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/180) / [#193](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/193) |
| Cold | Never an issue when temperate and dry; when cold or wet, a cloak or a fire within about 300 turns. WET 2×, SOAKED 3× heat loss. Field fire about 100 turns, radius 2. | [#181](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/181) / [#194](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/194) |

## Loot and economy ([#160](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/160), work in [#195](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/195), [#196](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/196), [#197](https://github.com/bluephoenixmedia/Tarmin2Revised/issues/197))

| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| Weapon floor | every weapon except the Father's Sword averages at least 3.5 (1d4 becomes 1d4+1) |
| Early weapon | a guaranteed one in the first chunk of every Incarnation |
| Per depth-1 expedition | about 1 ration per 2 expeditions; herbs for about 1 Poultice; about 1 healing potion per 2 expeditions; 1-2 Tinder Bundles; cloaks common |
| Gold | NetHack rarity: floor piles in about a third of rooms, `rnd(depth+2) × rnd(30)`; some monsters carry gold |
| Divinity | 15-25 per depth-1 expedition; first station after expedition 1-2 |
